Our Story

Born and raised in rural Bihar, I witnessed firsthand the challenges faced by farmers in accessing modern technology, fair markets, and timely information. This personal experience inspired me to create FarmLink AI.

With a background in Computer Science and AI, I combined my technical expertise with deep understanding of agricultural challenges to build a platform that truly serves farmers' needs.

"Technology should bridge gaps, not create them. FarmLink AI ensures that every farmer, regardless of their location or resources, has access to the same cutting-edge tools used by large agricultural corporations."
